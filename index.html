By proposition

The same coefficient fitted within each proposition separately. Each participant sees a given proposition at most once, so these are ordinary least squares with the document-set fixed effects retained — β is still identified from the randomised within-pair contrast. Bars are 95% intervals; with roughly a tenth of the data behind each, they are wide.
